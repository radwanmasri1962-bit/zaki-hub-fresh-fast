# Zaki Digital Hub

Create a premium, modern, mobile-first website for Zaki Mediterranean Grill, a Columbus, Ohio halal Mediterranean food business operating three food trucks plus a permanent location at Budd Dairy Food Hall.

I will upload the existing Zaki logo separately. Use the uploaded logo exactly as supplied. Do not redesign, reinterpret, recreate, or modify the logo.

The objective is not simply to create a restaurant website.

The website should become the central digital hub for Zaki, connecting customers to:

Order food

Find today's food trucks

View the menu

Visit Budd Dairy

Book a food truck

Request catering

Contact Zaki

The site should immediately make it easy for a customer to answer three questions:

I want Zaki now → ORDER ONLINE

Where is Zaki today? → FIND OUR TRUCKS

I need Zaki for an event → CATERING & FOOD TRUCK BOOKING

DESIGN REQUIREMENTS

The website must feel significantly more professional than a typical independent food-truck website.

However, do not make it look like a national corporate restaurant chain.

It should still feel:

Local. Human. Independent. Energetic. Food-driven.

Use the green brand confidently.

Use warm cream backgrounds to soften the experience.

Use charcoal typography for readability.

Use the warm gold only as an accent.

Use subtle rounded corners.

Avoid excessive gradients.

Avoid excessive animations.

Avoid glassmorphism.

Avoid generic AI-generated restaurant graphics.

Food photography will eventually be the visual hero.

The design must communicate that Zaki has grown beyond being simply a food truck while preserving its food-truck roots.

Most importantly:

Design the website around customer actions and restaurant revenue, not around decoration.
